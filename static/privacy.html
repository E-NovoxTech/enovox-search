Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Enovox Search, a technology discovery platform operated by E-NovoxTech.

Enovox Search helps people discover apps, AI tools, software, and digital products built by Nigerian developers, startups, and companies.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Enovox Search, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Company or organization name
  • Product information
  • Product website and links
  • Product category, pricing, and platform
  • Founder information
  • Other information you provide when submitting a product

We may also collect basic technical information, such as your browser, device type, IP address, and how you interact with our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Review and process product submissions.
  • Create and manage product listings.
  • Contact developers about their submissions.
  • Help users discover relevant products.
  • Improve Enovox Search and its features.
  • Provide and improve AI-powered search.
  • Protect the platform against spam, abuse, and security threats.

4. Public Product Information

Information submitted for a product listing may be displayed publicly on Enovox Search.

This can include the product name, description, company, founder information, website, pricing, category, platform, logo, and other product details.

Please do not submit information you do not want publicly displayed.

5. AI-Powered Search

Enovox Search uses artificial intelligence to help users discover relevant products.

Search queries may be processed by third-party AI providers to generate recommendations and improve the search experience.

AI recommendations may not always be accurate, so users should verify important information before making decisions.

6. Information Sharing

We may share limited information with trusted service providers that help us operate Enovox Search, including hosting, database, AI, analytics, and security providers.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect our users, platform, or rights.

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Third-Party Links

Enovox Search contains links to external websites and products.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing them with personal information.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.

For privacy-related requests, contact us using the email below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Enovox Search grows or our practices change.

When we make changes, we will update the Last Updated date on this page.
