Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Enovox Search.

What can I find on Enovox Search?

You can discover Nigerian-built AI tools, software, apps, SaaS products, digital platforms, and other technology products across different categories.

How do I find a product for a specific problem?

Use the search bar and describe what you're looking for. You can also use Ask AI to describe your needs in your own words and get relevant product suggestions.

Are all the products on Enovox Search made in Nigeria?

We focus on products built by Nigerian developers, startups, and companies. Product information is reviewed before listings are published.

Can I trust the information on a product listing?

We make reasonable efforts to keep listings accurate, but product features, pricing, availability, and other details can change. Always verify important information on the product's official website.

Can I submit a product I built?

Yes. Nigerian developers, founders, startups, and companies can submit eligible products. Submit a Product

Is it free to discover products on Enovox Search?

Yes. Searching and exploring products on Enovox Search is free.

Does Enovox Search own the products listed?

No. Enovox Search is a discovery platform. The products belong to their respective developers or companies.

How can I suggest a product that isn't listed?

You can submit the product, if you have the information. You can also contact the Enovox Search team if you know of a Nigerian product that should be discovered.

What is the goal of Enovox Search?

To make Nigerian technology easier to discover — so people can find products built here, while Nigerian builders get more visibility for what they're creating.
