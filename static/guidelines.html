Developer Guidelines

Build it. Submit it. Get discovered.

Enovox Search helps people discover Nigerian-built apps, AI tools, software, and digital products. These guidelines explain how to list and manage your product on our platform.

    1Why List Your Product?

    Increase Visibility

    Help more people discover your product.

    Reach New Users

    Connect your solution with potential users.

    Showcase Your Work

    Put your product in front of people interested in Nigerian technology.

    Join the Ecosystem

    Be part of Nigeria's growing technology community.

    Listing does not guarantee traffic, customers, or sales.

    2Who Can Submit?

    We welcome Nigerian developers, founders, startups, and companies building products for local or international markets.

    Your product doesn't have to serve only Nigerians, but it should meet our Nigerian-built technology focus.

    4How to Submit Your Product

    1. Visit the Submit Product page.
    2. Complete the form with accurate product information.
    3. Submit your product for review.
    4. Wait for the review decision.
    5. Once approved, your product becomes discoverable on Enovox Search.
    Submitting a product does not guarantee approval.

    5Review & Approval

    Every submission is reviewed before publication.

    We consider

    • Relevance to Enovox Search.
    • Accuracy of submitted information.
    • Accessibility of the product website.
    • Compliance with our guidelines.

    Possible outcomes

    Approved Pending Rejected
    Review times may vary.

    6Managing Your Product

    Already listed? Use the Developer Dashboard to manage your product. You can update supported information, including:

    • Product name and description.
    • Website and pricing.
    • Category and platform.
    • Other editable listing details.
    Keep your listing accurate. Misleading information or repeated violations may result in changes to your listing or its removal.

    7Product Listing Guidelines

    To maintain a useful discovery platform:

    • Provide truthful and accurate information.
    • Use your official product name and website.
    • Choose the most relevant category.
    • Keep your product description clear.
    • Update outdated information through your dashboard.
    • Don't submit duplicate listings for the same product without a valid reason.
    Enovox Search may reject or remove listings that violate these guidelines.

    8Frequently Asked Questions

    Can I submit multiple products?

    Yes. Each product must meet our guidelines.

    Can I submit a beta product?

    Yes, if it's sufficiently developed for users to understand or access it. Clearly indicate its beta status.

    Can I edit my listing after approval?

    Yes. Use your Developer Dashboard to update supported details.

    Does listing guarantee sales?

    No. Enovox Search helps people discover products but cannot guarantee customers or revenue.

    What if my product is rejected?

    Review the requirements, correct any issues, and resubmit if the option is available.

    Can international users discover my product?

    Yes. Nigerian-built products serving global markets are welcome.
